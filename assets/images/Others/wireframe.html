Controls Summary

Movement Wheel: Move & Jump (Arrow Keys)
Z (Punch Button): Punch attack
X (Kick Button): Kick attack
C (Ranged Button): Use ranged weapon
V (Magic Button): Cast magic spell
Combos: Chain punch + kick attacks
